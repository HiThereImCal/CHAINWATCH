Why Gas Matters for Builders
Every on-chain operation consumes gas. At 15 Gwei on mainnet, a simple ETH transfer costs ~£0.50. A complex DeFi interaction may cost £5–20. This directly affects your product's UX and unit economics. Base L2 reduces costs by 10–100× — the same transfer costs <£0.01. Gas prices fluctuate with network demand; the figures above update every 15 seconds.

Context for Founders
DeFi yields are variable and carry smart contract risk. Higher APY typically means higher risk — liquidity pool rewards fluctuate with trading volume, and lending rates shift with utilisation. The Belfast–Dublin corridor is home to growing teams building regulated DeFi products that bridge these yields into compliant financial infrastructure. Stablecoin lending (e.g. USDC on Aave) offers lower but more predictable returns with no price exposure.

Why Base?
Base is a Layer 2 built by Coinbase on the OP Stack. It inherits Ethereum's security while reducing gas costs by 10–100× and confirming transactions in ~2 seconds. For founders: Base gives you Ethereum-compatible smart contracts at a fraction of the cost, backed by Coinbase's infrastructure and distribution. The Belfast–Dublin blockchain community has direct connections to the Base ecosystem — an ideal deployment target for consumer-facing dApps.
